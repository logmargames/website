In-App Purchases

MagicBridge offers optional in-app purchases. Previously purchased content can be restored by selecting “Restore Purchases” in the MagicBridge settings.

Payments are processed by the respective app store. Logmar Games does not receive credit card or bank account information.

Problems or Questions

If a purchase is not recognized, content is not unlocked, or another technical issue occurs, please send us a short description of the problem together with your device model and operating system. Please do not send passwords or payment information.
