2. General Information

Thank you for your interest in our app MagicBridge and our website.

This Privacy Policy explains which data is processed when you use the app and visit this website, the purposes of such processing, and the rights available to you.

MagicBridge does not require registration or the creation of a user account. MagicBridge contains no advertising and does not use processed data for advertising or cross-app tracking purposes.

3. Data Stored Locally

MagicBridge stores information on the device that is required for gameplay and app functions. This includes in particular:

  • game progress
  • levels reached
  • stars collected
  • unlocked and purchased content
  • audio and language settings
  • calibration data
  • local Gabor test logs required for the game mechanics

This gameplay and settings data remains locally on the device and is not transmitted to Logmar Games or Unity.

4. In-App Purchases and Unity In-App Purchasing

MagicBridge offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payments are processed by the respective app store provider. Logmar Games and Unity do not receive credit card, bank account, or other direct payment information.

MagicBridge uses the Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity IAP) software module provided by Unity Technologies for the technical provision, execution, validation, and restoration of in-app purchases.

Unity IAP may process the following data in particular:

  • technical installation, device, and session identifiers
  • device model, operating system, and app, Unity, and IAP versions
  • country or region, particularly based on technical connection or store information; precise GPS location is not collected
  • product, purchase, transaction, and restoration information
  • technical diagnostic, error, and performance information relating to the IAP service

This data is used for app functionality, the technical handling of in-app purchases, troubleshooting, service security, and analysis of the IAP function. It is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

5. Recipients and Third-Party Providers

In connection with in-app purchases, data may be transmitted to or processed by the following providers:

  • Apple Inc. when using the Apple App Store
  • Google LLC when using Google Play
  • Unity Technologies as the provider of Unity In-App Purchasing

Logmar Games does not operate its own servers for MagicBridge and does not receive users’ personal gameplay, device, or payment data. Aggregated information about sales and app performance may be made available to Logmar Games through the platforms of the providers listed above.

Further information is available in the providers’ privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy

These providers may also process data in countries outside the European Economic Area. In such cases, transfers are governed by the providers’ applicable legal transfer mechanisms and privacy terms.

6. Advertising, Tracking, and Analytics

MagicBridge contains no advertising and does not use advertising networks. No cross-app tracking for advertising purposes takes place, and no personal user profiles are created for advertising purposes.

Unity IAP nevertheless processes the technical, purchase-related, and diagnostic information described in Section 4 for app functionality and analysis. This processing is not equivalent to advertising tracking.

7. Device Permissions and Location

MagicBridge does not request permission to access the following device features:

  • precise GPS location
  • camera
  • microphone
  • contacts

The approximate country or region information described in Section 4 is processed without access to precise GPS location.

8. Legal Bases

Where processing is necessary to provide the app and to execute or restore in-app purchases, it is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Where technical diagnostic, security, and performance data is processed, it is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest lies in providing the app securely and reliably, troubleshooting errors, and preventing misuse of in-app purchases.

Where statutory retention or documentation obligations apply, processing is based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

9. Retention Period

Locally stored gameplay and settings data remains on the device until the app is uninstalled, the app storage is deleted, or the relevant data is reset within the app.

Data processed by Apple, Google, or Unity is subject to the respective providers’ retention periods. According to Unity, service log data is generally retained for 90 days. Transaction data may be retained for longer in accordance with contractual or statutory requirements.

10. Use by Children

MagicBridge does not request names, email addresses, dates of birth, or other direct contact details. No user account is created.

The technical data processing by Unity IAP described in Section 4 may occur regardless of the user’s age, particularly when the app is launched or the in-app purchase function is used. Parents and legal guardians may contact the address specified in Section 1 with questions or to exercise data protection rights.

11. Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, and where the applicable legal requirements are met, you have the following rights in particular:

  • access
  • rectification
  • erasure
  • restriction of processing
  • data portability
  • objection to processing

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

For privacy questions or requests, please contact:
magicbridge.support@gmail.com

Where Unity processes data on behalf of Logmar Games, relevant requests may be forwarded to Unity through Logmar Games. Unity also provides unity-iap-contact@unity3d.com for inquiries relating to Unity IAP.

12. Provision of This Website

This website is provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub. When a GitHub Pages website is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored by GitHub for security purposes.

Processing related to the technical provision of this website is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the secure and reliable provision of our website. Logmar Games does not use its own analytics, advertising, or tracking services on this website.

Further information is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if legal requirements, services used, or app or website functions change. The current version published on this page applies.
